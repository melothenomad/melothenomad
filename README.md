### Hi, I'm Matheus.

Matheus Melo, known on social media as [@melothenomad](https://x.com/melothenomad), is a marketing strategist working with Web3 and fintech. He combines market research, creative thinking, and data to build marketing strategies focused on business growth.

I believe good marketing starts with understanding the business. What are we trying to achieve? Who are we trying to reach? And what needs to happen to get there?

From there, I research the market, define the strategy, and build a marketing plan that connects positioning, messaging, channels, and campaigns to clear business goals.

Data plays a key role throughout the process. I use it to understand performance, test ideas, and make better decisions as the strategy evolves.
